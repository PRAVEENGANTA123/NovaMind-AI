President’s Message

"I grew up at a time when India was a young country facing many challenges especially in the areas of education and healthcare. I made a quiet promise to myself that if I prosper in my professional endeavour, a major share of my earnings would be used for education and healthcare. In the last years I feel privileged and proud to see those dreams coming true as it also served to serve my social goals of taking care of economically weaker and underprivileged and weaker sections of society who needed best in class facilities in and around south Gujarat. We have a chain of schools today with cohorts of over 45000 students and I am especially proud if the giant strides we have made in the field of higher education. In just nine years PP Savani University has already emerged as one of Gujarat's best universities driving fourteen multidisciplinary schools, excellent faculty and an evolving vision to redefine the paradigm of excellence in education.


Industry 4.0 today present new challenges in education. We pre-empted why this and I am glad our academic deliveries are keeping pace with the times. The future of PP Savani student is in good hands. And it will remain so as the years unfold."


Jai Hind
